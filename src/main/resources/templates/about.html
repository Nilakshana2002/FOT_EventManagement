About Us

Empowering Innovation through Advanced Facilities and Seamless Event Coordination at the Faculty of Technology.

About the System

The University Event Management System is a state-of-the-art centralized platform developed exclusively for the Faculty of Technology, University of Ruhuna.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between world-class academic infrastructure and the needs of modern organizations. Whether you are hosting an international seminar, a hands-on workshop, or a corporate meeting, our system ensures a friction-less experience from booking to execution.

By digitizing the venue booking process, we enhance transparency, accessibility, and operational efficiency, allowing you to focus on what truly matters: your event's success.

Our Purpose

To provide a high-performance environment for academic and professional growth. We streamline coordination, simplify logistics, and offer a transparent platform that serves both internal university members and external corporate partners with equal excellence.

Who Can Use

Internal Community: Students, lecturers, and faculty clubs can access specialized spaces for academic projects and extracurricular activities.

External Partners: Corporate organizations, NGOs, and professional bodies are welcome to utilize our premium venues for workshops and seminars.
